Too Big Too Miss

Nike - Erling Haaland Player Edition

How do you stop a larger-than-life goal scoring machine from coming to your city and destroying your club? YOU DON'T! He’s a force of nature, an unstoppable giant of the game, and he’s coming to take over your city. Erling doesn’t just dominate, he towers over the opposition. Too Big To Miss.


Year

2025

Timeline

12 Weeks

Role

Art Direction + Design

Creative Approach

In the Summer of 2025, we sought to release the next big blockbuster movie featuring the larger-than-life athlete, Erling Haaland.

By building a custom miniature city and enhancing the realism in CGI, we were able to place a giant Erling in a world where he is set to destroy his competition.

To further amplify the concept, we shot Erling's Player Edition boot on a trailer of a 1:24 scale model of a British lorry. We used movie graphics such as poster textures, critic reviews and end credits to tie our two distinct visual worlds.



Make it Real

"Too Big Too Miss" was a simple idea with an endless runway of exploration and world building possibilities. Our partners in Nike Europe took this basic concept and made it a reality. Ultimately building a size 672 UK football boot, tying it down to the back of a real British lorry, and driving the massive replica around Manchester, UK ahead of the big derby game.

My Responsibilities

Led art direction and design for in-studio product photography, supported athlete photography post production, contributed to early conceptual narrative direction, and led art direction for digital design deliverables.
